          Quick start
          
    
            
      
          1. Add notes. Paste or type them in Meeting notes, choose Load example…, or use Open file for a .txt or .md file.
            2. 
      
          2. Extract actions. Choose Extract actions or press Ctrl+Enter.
            3. 
      
          3. Review. Fix anything the yellow banner flags: actions without an owner or a due date.
            4. 
      
          4. Share. Choose Share, pick a format, then Copy.
            5. 
    
          

    
          What you see after extracting
          
    
            
      
          • Summary cards show how many action items, decisions, follow-ups and risks were found. Click a card to see that list.
            • 
      
          • Highlighted notes (left) colour every line that produced an item: blue for actions, green for decisions, amber for follow-ups and red for risks. Click a coloured line to open its item; click Line n on an item to jump back to the note.
            • 
      
          • Who has what shows how many actions each person owns.
            • 
    
          

    
          Editing an action
          
    
            
      
          • Task: click the text and type.
            • 
      
          • Owner: click the name chip and pick or type a name. A dashed yellow chip means no owner yet.
            • 
      
          • Due date: click the date chip to open a calendar. The quoted word, such as “Friday”, shows what the notes said.
            • 
      
          • Priority: click High, Medium or Low to change it.
            • 
      
          • Remove: use the bin icon on the right of the row. You can undo it straight away.
            • 
      
          • Add: choose Add an action under the list.
            • 
    
          

    
          Writing notes that work well
          
    
          Put the owner's name before the task (“Priya to…”, “@Ravi…”, “Sana: …”), add a date word (“by Friday”, “EOD”, “11 Dec”), and include a Date: line and an Attendees: line at the top. Open How to write notes so nothing is missed above the notes box for more examples.
          

    
          Keyboard shortcuts
          
    
          
      
          Ctrl+EnterExtract actions
          
      
          EscClose a window or the tour
          
      
          TabMove between fields
          
    
          

    
          Privacy
          
    
          In Standard mode everything happens in your browser and your notes are never sent anywhere. Your current notes are kept in this browser so a refresh doesn't lose them; Clear removes them. AI mode sends notes to the Anthropic API using your own key.
